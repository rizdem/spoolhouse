Settings

How the app runs, and keeping your data safe.

These options are only available in the app, not in a browser.

Keep tracking in the menu bar
Closing the window leaves the app running from its icon in the menu bar, so printer usage keeps coming off your stock. Use Quit (⌘Q) to stop it completely.
Open at login
Starts quietly in the menu bar when you log in to your Mac, so tracking never stops.

Notifications are available in the app, not in a browser.

CFS humidity limit
Warn when a CFS unit's humidity goes above this. Most filament prints best kept below 40–50%.

# Security

Spoolhouse runs on your own computer and phone, keeps everything there, and talks only to your printer,
your NFC reader and (for update checks) GitHub. If you find a way it could be misused, for example
something that lets another device on your network read or change your inventory through Phone access,
please tell me privately first.

## Reporting a problem

Use **Report a vulnerability** on the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/rizdem/spoolhouse/security/advisories/new). Only I can see the report.
Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

Include what you did, what happened, and the Spoolhouse version (shown in About). I'll reply within a
week, and credit you in the release notes when it's fixed, if you'd like.

## Versions

Fixes go into the newest release; Spoolhouse tells you when one is out (Settings → Updates).
